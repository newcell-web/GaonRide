About GaonRide AI

In rural Assam and across India, millions of villagers rely on infrequent and unreliable shared transport to reach towns for work, healthcare, and markets. Fixed schedules and empty seats waste resources while passengers wait for hours. GaonRide AI aggregates travel demand in real-time, uses artificial intelligence to group passengers by route, and connects them with local drivers — so every seat is filled, every trip is worth it, and no one is left stranded at a roadside.
